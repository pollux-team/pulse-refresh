Privacy Policy — PulseRefresh

Effective Date: November 17, 2026 · PulseRefresh – Auto Refresh & Page Monitor
Summary: PulseRefresh is a local-first, self-contained browser extension. We do not collect, store, sell, rent, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, cookies, page content, analytics, or telemetry to any developer-operated server.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

PulseRefresh stores your settings exclusively in your browser's built-in extension storage (storage.local and, when enabled, storage.sync):

  • Refresh intervals, countdown overlay preferences, and saved profiles
  • Active tab job state, local page comparison snapshots, and a clearable activity log
  • Auto-start URL rules, URL rotation lists, keyword/expression rules, and templates
  • Automation macros, user scripts, keyboard shortcuts, and domain-scoped cookie rules
  • Optional webhook URLs, email API credentials, custom sound URLs, or AI provider API keys you explicitly enter
  • A friendly browser and platform label (such as Chrome · macOS) used solely to identify your own synced browsers

When Browser Sync is enabled, your browser vendor (Google, Mozilla, or Microsoft) syncs your chosen settings across your signed-in devices under that vendor's privacy policy. PulseRefresh has no access to your account.

2. How Permissions & Page Data Are Used

  • Page Monitoring: Page text and element checks run strictly locally in your browser. In Silent XHR mode, your browser fetches the monitored URL directly to compare updated content without flickering the tab.
  • Interaction & Form Protection: Local click, scroll, and form-state listeners are used only to pause or skip a scheduled reload when enabled. Keystrokes are never logged or transmitted.
  • Cookies: Cookie rules read, clear, preserve, or locally snapshot only cookies matching domains you explicitly configure. Cookies are never transmitted externally.
  • Power Management: Used only while the optional Prevent System Sleep toggle and at least one refresh job are active.

3. Optional User-Configured Outbound Connections

External network requests occur solely to destinations you configure:

  • The webpages you explicitly choose to reload or monitor via XHR
  • Optional Slack, Discord, Telegram, or custom HTTPS webhook URLs you add
  • Optional email delivery APIs (Resend, SendGrid, EmailJS, or custom HTTPS relay) using your own credentials
  • Optional OpenAI-compatible endpoint when you enter an API key and generate an automation script (offline mode makes zero network calls)
  • Optional custom notification audio URL if configured

4. No Analytics, Ads, or Data Sharing

PulseRefresh contains zero analytics libraries, zero advertising or affiliate scripts, zero remote code execution, and never sells or shares user data with third parties.

5. Data Control & Deletion

You can export your settings as JSON, clear logs, or delete any rule or credential at any time inside PulseRefresh Settings. Uninstalling the extension immediately removes all locally stored extension data.

6. Contact

For privacy inquiries or support, contact support@pulserefresh.app (replace with your active support email or repository support URL before publishing).
