At a glance

The short version

PulseRefresh is local-first and self-contained. We do not collect, store, sell, rent, or transmit your personal data, browsing history, cookies, page content, analytics, or telemetry to any developer-operated server.

Policy details

PulseRefresh stores your settings exclusively in your browser's built-in extension storage (storage.local and, when you enable it, storage.sync):

  • Refresh intervals, countdown overlay preferences, and saved profiles
  • Active tab job state, local page comparison snapshots, and a clearable activity log
  • Auto-start URL rules, URL rotation lists, keyword/expression rules, and templates
  • Automation macros, user scripts, keyboard shortcuts, and domain-scoped cookie rules
  • Optional webhook URLs, email API credentials, custom sound URLs, or AI provider API keys you enter yourself
  • A friendly browser and platform label (such as Chrome · macOS) used solely to identify your own synced browsers

When Browser Sync is enabled, your browser vendor (Google, Mozilla, or Microsoft) syncs your chosen settings across signed-in devices under that vendor's own privacy policy. PulseRefresh has no access to your account.

  • Page monitoring: Page text and element checks run strictly locally in your browser. In Silent XHR mode, your browser fetches the monitored URL directly to compare updated content without flickering the visible tab.
  • Interaction & form protection: Local click, scroll, and form-state listeners are used only to pause or skip a scheduled reload when enabled. Keystrokes are never logged or transmitted.
  • Cookies: Cookie rules read, clear, preserve, or locally snapshot only cookies matching domains you explicitly configure. Cookies are never transmitted externally.
  • Power management: Used only while the optional Prevent System Sleep toggle and at least one refresh job are active.

External network requests occur solely to destinations you configure:

  • The webpages you explicitly choose to reload or monitor via XHR
  • Optional Slack, Discord, Telegram, or custom HTTPS webhook URLs you add
  • Optional email delivery APIs (Resend, SendGrid, EmailJS, or a custom HTTPS relay) using your own credentials
  • An optional OpenAI-compatible endpoint when you enter an API key and generate a script — offline mode makes zero network calls
  • An optional custom notification audio URL, if configured

Any third-party service you connect is governed by its own privacy policy.

  • No third-party analytics SDKs — no Google Analytics, Sentry, Mixpanel, or tracking pixels
  • No advertisements, affiliate link injection, or monetization scripts
  • No sale, licensing, or transfer of user data to any third party
  • No remote code execution

You stay in control of all extension data at all times:

  • Export or back up your configuration as a JSON file from Settings → Profiles & Sync
  • Clear your activity log with one click from Settings → Activity Log
  • Remove individual profiles, rules, cookie snapshots, webhooks, or API keys from Settings
  • Uninstalling PulseRefresh automatically and permanently deletes all local extension storage from your browser

PulseRefresh is a general-purpose productivity utility and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

If this policy is updated to reflect new extension functionality, the revised policy and updated effective date will be published at the same URL before the corresponding extension update takes effect.
